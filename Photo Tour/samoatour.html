Welcome to Samoa

Welcome to my photo tour of Samoa! This website explores five beautiful places, including the capital city, natural swimming pools, waterfalls, and peaceful island scenery. Each stop shows what makes Samoa special and gives visitors a chance to learn about its natural beauty and culture.

Explore the Five Stops

  • Apia — The capital of Samoa
  • To-Sua Ocean Trench
  • Piula Cave Pool
  • Papaseea Sliding Rocks
  • Manono Island

Respect Samoan Culture

When visiting Samoa, respect village customs, dress appropriately, ask permission before photographing people, and follow local safety advice.
